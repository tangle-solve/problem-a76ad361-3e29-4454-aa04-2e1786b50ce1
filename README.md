### CHEM-000388: Unknown side products that divert irradiated chromium recombination
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Identify the accumulating products that cause Cr(I)/Cr(III) recombination in molten LiCl–KCl to depart from second-order kinetics, and determine their reaction pathways.
- **Status:** Pulse-radiolysis measurements resolved primary reactions, but increasing accumulated dose introduced competing chemistry whose products remained unidentified.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Reveals when reversible radiation chemistry produces persistent changes in a molten salt.
- **Key works:** K. Iwamatsu et al. (2026; first published 2025), “Kinetics of radiation-induced Cr(II) and Cr(III) redox chemistry in molten LiCl–KCl eutectic,” [DOI: 10.1039/D4CP04190A](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4CP04190A).
- **Related:** Radiation physics—cumulative-dose effects that invalidate independent-event models.
